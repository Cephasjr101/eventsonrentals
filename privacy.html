Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Events-On Rentals ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect on this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact form details — your name, phone number and message, used only to respond to your enquiry.
  • Privacy-friendly analytics — if you accept, we count page visits (page path and time only). No cookies, no IP logging, no cross-site tracking.

What we don't do

  • We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not store form submissions on this website — messages are handed to WhatsApp so they reach us directly.

WhatsApp

When you contact us on WhatsApp, your messages are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We use your number only to respond to your enquiry.

Your choices

You can accept or decline analytics at any time via the consent banner, or clear your choice by clearing your browser's site data. Declining analytics does not affect your use of the site.
